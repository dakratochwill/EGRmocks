Course Highlights

A few courses that set this program apart.

EGRB 101 / 301

Biomedical Engineering Practicum Series

Participate in medical rounds at VCU Medical Center's MCV Hospitals, research labs across the Virginia BioTechnology Research Park, and clinical case studies — a hands-on opportunity unlike any other program in the nation.

EGRB 310

Biomechanics

Study the forces, stresses and strains in the human body, analyzing hard and soft tissue from the perspective of structure and function using core engineering mechanics.

EGRB 427

Biomaterials

Study materials used in implants, prostheses and medical devices, analyzing the physical, chemical and physiological factors that determine how they perform in the human body.
